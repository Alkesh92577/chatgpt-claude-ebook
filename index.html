The 5-Part Prompt Formula

Anything you leave out, the AI has to guess. R-C-T-F-L gives it what it needs.

R

Role

Who the AI should act as

C

Context

Background, audience, goal

T

Task

Exactly what to do

F

Format

How the answer should look

L

Limits

Length, tone, things to avoid

WEAK PROMPT

“Help me with marketing.”

STRONG PROMPT

Role: Act as a marketing assistant for a small local yoga studio. Context: we have 40 members and want 15 more, our audience is women aged 30 to 50, and our budget is $100 a month. Task: suggest 5 low-cost ways to get new members. Format: a table with columns for idea, cost, effort and expected result. Limits: no paid ads, and keep each cell under 12 words.

Don't Just Read About AI. Build the Habit.

Ten to fifteen minutes a day is enough. Consistency beats intensity.

Week 1: Get comfortable

Set up both accounts. Ask 2 real questions a day. Practice follow-ups: shorter, warmer, simpler.

Week 2: Prompt like a pro

Use R-C-T-F-L on every request. Try 5 prompts from the library. Save your favorites.

Week 3: Go deeper

Upload a file. Create a Project. Try memory, web search and one research task. Compare ChatGPT vs. Claude.

Week 4: Make it a habit

Automate one weekly task. Write your own 5 personal prompts. Decide whether a paid plan is worth it.
